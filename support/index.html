Frequently asked questions

How do I reset my progress?
Open the app, go to Settings, and use the account or progress reset options. If you need help, email us.
Is Passion Seed free?
Yes, the core career exploration experience is free to use.
Can I use the app on iPad?
Yes, Passion Seed supports both iPhone and iPad.
How do I delete my account?
Go to Settings in the app and choose "Delete Account". This will permanently remove your data.
