# Top 100 Jobs Report: Security, Trends, Salary, AI Impact & Future Evolution

**Date:** May 1, 2026
**Prepared by:** PassionSeed Research

---

## Executive Summary

This report ranks and analyzes the top 100 jobs across 10 categories, evaluating each on 7 dimensions: demand, salary (global + Thailand), growth projection, AI automation risk, job security, future-proof score, and how the role will evolve by 2030–2035.

**Key findings:**
- Net **+78 million jobs** globally by 2030 (WEF) — 170M created, 92M displaced
- **76% of employees** now use AI at work, up from 30% in 2023 (McKinsey)
- Entry-level workers ages 22–25 seeing **13–16% employment declines** in AI-exposed roles (Stanford/Goldman Sachs)
- White-collar knowledge work is MORE affected by AI than blue-collar — reversing historical automation patterns
- **72% of employers globally** report hiring difficulty (ManpowerGroup 2026)
- Thailand EdTech market: **USD 1.76B** (2025) → **USD 4.89B** by 2034
- Thailand salary increases: **4.5–4.7%** annually; tech roles command **15–30% premiums**

### Scoring System

Each job is rated on:
- **AI Risk:** 🟢 Low / 🟡 Medium / 🔴 High — probability of significant automation
- **Job Security:** ⭐⭐⭐ (high) to ⭐ (low) — recession-proof, essential, hard to replace
- **Future-Proof:** ⭐⭐⭐ (high) to ⭐ (low) — long-term career viability through 2035
- **Growth:** BLS/WEF projected growth rate
