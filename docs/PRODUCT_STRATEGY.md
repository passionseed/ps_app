**Status:** Draft — founder review

## Executive Summary

Passion Seed is a mobile-first career discovery platform for Thai high school students navigating university admissions. We don't tell students what to do. We build a safe space where they can taste different paths, reflect on what actually energizes them, and walk away with a concrete plan they trust.

**The core insight:** Thai students don't lack ambition. They lack *permission to explore* and *data to decide*. Our competitors either give them dry information (Dek-D) or expensive hand-holding (consultants). We slot in between: gamified, affordable, self-directed — but structured enough that a lost student isn't staring at a blank page.

**Three personas. Three features. One platform.**

**Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM):** ~400,000 M.6 students + ~800,000 M.4–M.5 students actively exploring paths = **~1.2M students at any given time**

**Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM):**  
Year 1 target: 10,000 active users (0.8% SAM)  
Year 2 target: 50,000 active users (4% SAM)  
Year 3 target: 150,000 active users (12.5% SAM)

### Revenue Model

| Tier | Price | What You Get |
|------|-------|--------------|
| **Free** | ฿0 | Browse seeds, take 1 PathLab preview (2 days), Direction Finder |
| **Seed** | ฿99/month | Full PathLabs, Plan Cards, progress tracking |
| **Sprout** | ฿299/month | Portfolio builder, buddy matching, opportunity alerts, verified credentials |
| **Bloom** | ฿999/one-time | 1-on-1 coaching session + full plan review |

**Why this pricing works:** The gap between "free but useless" (Dek-D forums) and "expensive but effective" (consultants at ฿10,000+) is the exact space 80% of Thai families occupy.

### Why Now

1. **TCASFolio launching July 2026** — standardized portfolio submission kills the "pretty PDF" market. Value shifts to *content* and *verification*.
2. **TCAS Verified launching 2026–2027** — creates demand for platforms that can issue digital credentials.
3. **Government cracking down on paid portfolio fabrication** — students need legitimate paths to build real portfolios.
4. **No incumbent owns the end-to-end journey** — Dek-D is media, not tools. No one connects "I want this major" to "here's my verified portfolio."
5. **Mobile penetration 90%+** — the audience is reachable. We just need to meet them where they are.

## Persona 1: Orange Cat — The Lost Dreamer

> "Everyone else has a plan. I just have a list of maybes and a lot of fanfiction."

### Profile
- **Grade:** M.6 (Grade 12)
- **School:** Regular Thai high school, no special program
- **Current state:** Has a ranked list of interested majors but zero conviction
- **Coping mechanism:** Reads fanfiction to escape reality; fantasy feels safer than her actual future
- **Social dynamic:** Feels inferior to friends who already have university plans

### Goals (Prioritized)
1. Get into university
2. Find a university/major that actually suits her
3. Join activities to build portfolio
4. Get good grades
5. Find overseas opportunities

### Pain Points (Prioritized)
1. No confidence she can get into a good university
2. Lacks self-motivation — when she tries to research, she spirals into anxiety and avoidance
3. Never researches university info — doesn't know where to start
4. Blindly follows whatever friends or family say is "good"

### Needs (Prioritized)
1. Something to motivate her — not guilt, not pressure, but genuine spark
2. Beginner-friendly guidance that holds her hand through planning
3. A way to test which major actually fits, not which one sounds impressive

### Value Proposition
> **"Find your direction before you need to commit."**

We don't ask Orange Cat to pick a major on day one. We give her a 4-day "taste test" of a path — small enough that failure doesn't sting, structured enough that she isn't staring at a blank page. Every reflection gives her data about *herself* that she trusts more than her anxiety.

### Feature Mapping

| Feature | How It Helps Orange Cat | Activation Moment |
|---------|------------------------|-------------------|
| **Direction Finder** | Ranks majors by fit, explains *why* in her language | "Oh, Psychology ranked higher than Law because I said I like understanding people" |
| **PathLab Previews** (2-day free) | Low-commitment taste of a path — no enrollment pressure | Day 1 task is a 5-min interactive story, not a reading assignment |
| **Daily Reflections** | Surfaces her genuine interest vs. anxiety-driven "shoulds" | Sees her "energy" score on Nursing vs. Communications |
| **Plan Card** | Concrete, shareable action plan with deadlines | "I have a plan now. It's not just in my head." |
| **Anonymous Peer Reflections** | "Other people felt lost too" — motivation through relatability | Reads: "I was terrified too, but I tried this path and now I'm in my dream faculty" |

### Customer Journey

| Stage | What Happens | Touchpoint | Emotion |
|-------|-------------|------------|---------|
| **Awareness** | Sees a TikTok/Reels: "Find your major in 5 days without the pressure" | Social media content | Curious, skeptical |
| **Acquisition** | Downloads app, takes Direction Finder (5 min) | App store → onboarding | Surprised it's short |
| **Activation** | Gets ranked major list with "why" explanations; taps first PathLab preview | Direction Finder results | "This gets me" |
| **Engagement** | Completes Day 1 of PathLab (interactive story/video/reflection, ~10 min) | PathLab screen | Absorbed, safe |
| **Retention** | Day 2 reflection asks: "How did that make you feel?" She marks "energized." Sees her energy vs. confusion chart building. | Reflection modal | Self-awareness spark |
| **Revenue** | Hits preview limit, subscribes to unlock full paths + Plan Cards | Paywall | "It's 99 baht. I spend more on bubble tea." |
| **Referral** | Screenshots her Plan Card, posts to IG story: "Finally not panicking about uni" | Social share | Proud, relieved |

### Key Quote
> "I thought I had to know everything before I started. Passion Seed showed me I just had to start."

## Persona 2: Itim — The Pragmatic Builder

> "My school won't help me build a portfolio. So I'll do it myself — I just need to know what 'good' actually looks like."

### Profile
- **Grade:** M.5 (Grade 11)
- **School:** Regular Thai high school
- **Current state:** Has one ideal major, some interest in others. Portfolio-first strategy.
- **Mindset:** Skeptical, self-reliant, deadline-driven. Doesn't trust school guidance.
- **Strategy:** Skipped Round 1, targeting Round 2. Not confident he can build a strong portfolio in time.

### Goals (Prioritized)
1. Get into university
2. Join activities to build portfolio
3. Find friends to join contests with (collaboration for portfolio building)

### Pain Points (Prioritized)
1. No guidance on *how* to build a portfolio — what goes in it, what format, what faculty actually values
2. Blindly believes info he reads (gave up on Round 1 based on forum anecdotes, not data)
3. School has insufficient resources to support portfolio building

### Needs (Prioritized)
1. Beginner-friendly guidance that breaks portfolio building into concrete steps
2. Real data for planning — admission stats, what past successful applicants did, deadline realities
3. Community in the same field — people to team up with for contests, share resources, sanity-check plans

### Value Proposition
> **"Build a portfolio that actually gets you in."**

We turn portfolio building from a vague anxiety into a structured project with milestones, deadlines, and peer support. Itim gets real data on what his target faculty values, tracks his progress against actual admission timelines, and finds teammates for contests instead of posting into the void of Facebook groups.

### Feature Mapping

| Feature | How It Helps Itim | Activation Moment |
|---------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Portfolio Path Node** | PathLab includes actual "build" tasks: "Design thumbnail for mock campaign" | Sees a task that's output, not consumption |
| **Opportunities Tab** | Aggregated contest/camp/open calls with deadlines, filtered by faculty | "There's a design contest next month in my province" |
| **Buddy Matching** | Matched with another M.5 student interested in same field for contest collab | "I don't have to do this alone" |
| **Real Data Cards** | "Last year, 73% of Round 2 CommArts entrants had X portfolio type" | "This is actual data, not forum rumors" |
| **Deadline Tracker** | Round 1 vs Round 2 timeline with his personalized milestones | "I can still make Round 1 if I start now" |
| **Plan Card (Portfolio Edition)** | Faculty-specific portfolio checklist with verification status | "I can see exactly what's missing" |

## Persona 3: KFC — The Good Daughter in Conflict

> "My family says วิทย์สุข is safest. But when I read about business, I feel something I don't feel about nursing. Is that enough to bet on?"

### Profile
- **Grade:** M.5 (Grade 11)
- **School:** Regular Thai high school
- **Family context:** Entire family in วิทย์สุข (health sciences). Strong pressure to continue the lineage.
- **Personal interest:** Business, especially entrepreneurship. But "okay" with nursing as family-approved path.
- **Fallback plan:** If nursing doesn't work out, become air hostess. (Not a plan — a retreat.)
- **Information diet:** Almost entirely family. No external research habits.
- **Strategy:** Plans to focus on both portfolio and test prep. Has a plan, but it's built on one source.

### Goals (Prioritized)
1. Get into university
2. Join activities to build portfolio
3. Test prep for admission rounds

### Pain Points (Prioritized)
1. No guidance on portfolio building — her family's experience is test-heavy, not portfolio-era
2. Blindly believes family info — no independent verification
3. Doesn't get to enter the major she has most interest in — business feels like betrayal

### Needs (Prioritized)
1. Beginner-friendly guidance that helps her plan university entry independently
2. Real data to give her confidence to pursue her actual interest — not rebellion, just evidence
3. A way to explore business *safely* — without committing, without announcing to family

### Value Proposition
> **"Explore both paths with data, not just family pressure."**

We give KFC a private space to taste business without anyone knowing. She gets real salary data, demand projections, and admission pathways for both nursing and business. The Direction Finder becomes her secret second opinion. And when she's ready, the Plan Card gives her a side-by-side comparison she can actually use in a family conversation — not as rebellion, but as an informed choice.

### Feature Mapping

| Feature | How It Helps KFC | Activation Moment |
|---------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Direction Finder** | Business ranks higher than Nursing. The *system* says it, not her. | "The algorithm thinks I'd be happier in Business... that's not just me being difficult" |
| **Dual Path Previews** | Can preview both Nursing and Business paths without commitment or public enrollment | Enrolls in both preview paths secretly |
| **Reality Check Nodes** | Day-in-the-life content: nurse shift schedules vs. business analyst workflows | "Nursing is 12-hour shifts. Business is project deadlines. Both hard, but different hard." |
| **Data Cards** | Side-by-side: median salary, demand growth, AI risk for Nursing vs. Business | "Business has higher AI risk but also higher growth. I can see the trade-off." |
| **Comparison Plan Card** | "If Nursing:" timeline A. "If Business:" timeline B. With real admission data. | "I can show my mom the numbers. It's not just 'I want it.'" |
| **Shareable Summary** (optional) | Clean, factual summary she can share with family — no drama, just data | "Here's what I learned about both options" |
