# Phase 3 — Content Production Checklist

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** May 2026  
**Deadline:** May 11 (workshop) / May 22 (Round 1 submission)  
**Method:** Backward from Round 1. Parallel tracks where possible.

---

## Production Philosophy

1. **Start from the video.** Round 1 judge rubric defines what teams must produce. Work backward to the workspace steps that produce those outputs.
2. **Content before code.** Write all module copy, AI prompts, and form fields before building UI. Content is the product; code is the container.
3. **Parallel tracks.** Workshop, app modules, daily system, and grading pipeline can be built simultaneously by different people.
4. **Ship modules early.** Modules 1 + 2 must be live before May 11. Every day of delay pushes the workshop back.
5. **Test with one team.** Before May 12, run one complete cycle with a volunteer team. Fix gaps. Then open to all.

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| Module 1+2 not ready by May 10 | Blocks workshop | Priority #1. Cut video production if needed. |
