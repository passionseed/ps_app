# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Students and early-career professionals in education or recently graduated who are exploring potential career directions. Their context is often on-the-go exploration, seeking clarity and confidence about their future.

## Product Purpose
To empower the next generation to discover and navigate ideal career paths via a gamified, AI-driven career path simulation and exploration platform. It grounds simulations in real job market data and educational programs.

## Brand Personality
Clean, innovative, trustable.
(Note: The Hackathon section uses a distinct "bioluminescent/immersive" sub-theme).

## Anti-references
Microsoft (avoid corporate, dry, overly complex, or rigid enterprise UI patterns).

## Design Principles
- **Gamified Discovery**: Make career exploration engaging and interactive rather than a chore.
- **Data-Grounded Reality**: Surface job market and university data clearly so choices feel actionable and real.
- **Mobile-First Immersion**: Deliver a seamless, high-performance mobile experience with fluid transitions and satisfying haptics.
- **Dual-Thematic Flexibility**: Maintain a clean, bright primary interface while supporting immersive, cinematic sub-experiences (like the Hackathon).

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Standard mobile best practices (e.g., adequate touch targets and legibility). No specific strict constraints.
